Why Donate Blood?

  • One donation can save up to three lives.
  • Hospitals and emergency cases always need blood.
  • It promotes compassion and community well-being.
  • Giving blood is a powerful way to help others.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age between 18 and 65 years.
  • Minimum weight of 50 kg.
  • Hemoglobin level at least 12.5 g/dL.
  • No major illness or active infection at the time of donation.

Precautions Before Donation

  • Eat a light, healthy meal before donating.
  • Drink enough water or fluids before donation.
  • Avoid donating on an empty stomach.
  • Inform staff about recent travel or medications.

After Care Instructions

  • Drink plenty of water after donation.
  • Keep the bandage on for a few hours.
  • Avoid heavy lifting for 24 hours.
  • If dizzy, sit or lie down until you feel better.
